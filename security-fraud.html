Security & Fraud

Protect yourself from impersonation and fraud.

Fraudsters can imitate legitimate companies, websites, executives, documents, email addresses, and investment communications.

Stay Alert

Common warning signs.

Be suspicious of communications that create unusual urgency, request secrecy, change payment instructions unexpectedly, ask for passwords or authentication codes, or direct you to domains that only resemble Newmara's official website.

Impersonation

A fraudster may use a real company or employee name while communicating from an unrelated email address, social account, or website.

Payment fraud

Always independently verify new or changed wire instructions using a previously established, trusted contact method.

Credential theft

Never disclose passwords or multi-factor authentication codes in response to an unsolicited request.

Verify Newmara

Use official channels.

The official public website is newmara.com. If you receive a suspicious communication claiming to be from Newmara, navigate independently to the official website and contact us through the published form.

Newmara will not ask you to bypass normal verification procedures or provide your password or authentication code by email, text message, or telephone.
