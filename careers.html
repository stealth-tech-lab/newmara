Careers

Do work that changes the outcome.

We bring investing and operations together. We look for people who think like owners, get close to the details, and want responsibility for turning ideas into results.

Our Culture

High standards. Low ego. Real ownership.

We believe strong teams combine intellectual rigor with practical judgment. People should challenge assumptions, make decisions with incomplete information, and stay accountable for what happens next.

At Newmara, investing is not separated from execution. The work can span underwriting, diligence, strategy, operations, pricing, technology, reporting, real estate, and capital allocation.

Think like an owner

Understand the economics, customer, risk, and long-term consequence, not simply the task.

Stay close to the work

Good decisions come from understanding how a business or asset actually operates.

Raise the standard

Be rigorous, candid, curious, and willing to improve how the work gets done.

Your Career

Responsibility should grow with capability.

We want people to build judgment, not simply complete assignments. That means exposure to real decisions, direct collaboration, and opportunities to understand an investment from acquisition through operation and long-term ownership.

Investing

Evaluate businesses and real assets, support diligence, and help make disciplined capital decisions.

Operations

Work on initiatives that drive performance after closing, from reporting and systems to sales, pricing, and execution.

Real Estate

Support acquisitions, asset management, development, capital projects, leasing, and operating performance.

What We Value

Curiosity, judgment, accountability.

Credentials matter less than the quality of your thinking and the way you work. We value people who communicate clearly, learn quickly, care about details, and can move between strategic questions and practical execution.
