Our Firm

Built from an owner’s perspective.

Newmara was built around a belief that the most important work in investing often begins after the transaction closes.

Our Foundation

Investing informed by operations.

Our perspective comes from approaching assets as owners and operators. Financial discipline matters, but durable value is ultimately shaped by customers, people, systems, execution, capital allocation, and the decisions made every day.

That owner-operator mindset became the foundation for Newmara: combine disciplined investment judgment with the willingness to engage deeply in the assets we own.

Businesses

Partner with management and focus on the operating drivers that can strengthen a company over time.

Real Assets

Treat real estate as an operating business, with attention to basis, operations, capital projects, tenants, and cash flow.

Capital

Protect flexibility and allocate capital where it can create the greatest long-term impact.

Where We Are Going

Building an enduring investment platform.

Newmara is being built for long-term ownership rather than around a single transaction or market cycle. Our objective is to develop a platform capable of partnering with business owners, management teams, real estate partners, and long-term capital across multiple opportunities.

We intend to grow deliberately, preserve an owner's mentality, and keep investment decisions connected to operating reality.
