Student Careers

Learn investing by getting closer to the business.

The best early-career experience connects financial analysis with the real-world decisions that create value inside businesses and real assets.

Early Careers

Build the foundation.

Students and recent graduates should learn more than how to build a model. They should understand why a company wins customers, how management allocates resources, what makes an asset resilient, and how an owner turns an investment thesis into action.

Future student opportunities at Newmara are intended to provide exposure to both investment judgment and operating execution.

Learn the fundamentals

Develop practical skills in financial analysis, research, diligence, market assessment, and investment communication.

See the operating side

Understand how pricing, sales, people, technology, reporting, and capital allocation affect outcomes.

Think like an owner

Ask not only whether an investment is attractive, but what an owner can actually do to make it better.

The Experience

From analysis to action.

Depending on the opportunity, students may support live investment work, market research, operating analyses, real estate projects, portfolio-company initiatives, and internal strategic projects.

Mentorship

Learn directly from people working across investment and operating decisions.

Exposure

Gain perspective across private businesses, real assets, and the work that follows an acquisition.

Development

Build the judgment, communication, and analytical habits required for a career in investing and operations.
