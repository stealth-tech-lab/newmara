Business Owners

Your business. Your next chapter.

You built the business. What comes next should reflect what matters to you. Newmara works with owners considering a full sale, a gradual transition, or a partner to help grow the company.

Flexible Paths

There is more than one way to transition a business.

Some owners are ready to retire. Others want to stay involved for several years. Some are not ready to sell at all, but know the business needs additional capital, systems, people, or operating support to reach its next stage.

Our job is to understand what you want first, then determine whether there is a structure that works for both sides.

Full Acquisition

Ready to move on? Newmara can acquire the business and take responsibility for its next chapter while working to preserve the strengths that made it successful.

Gradual Succession

Step back over time. A transition can be structured around your desired involvement, continuity for employees and customers, and an orderly transfer of responsibility.

Growth Partnership

Keep meaningful ownership while adding a committed partner, capital, and hands-on operating support to help build the next stage of the business.

How We Work

We are investors who operate.

We are not looking to simply buy a company and watch from a distance. We work with leadership on the practical areas that influence performance: people, sales, pricing, customer experience, technology, systems, reporting, capital allocation, and growth.

The goal is not to replace everything that made a business successful. It is to understand what works, protect it, and improve the areas that can make the company stronger.

Listen first

Understand the owner, the business, the people, and what a successful transition actually means.

Build the plan

Agree on ownership, involvement, priorities, and the operating plan for the next chapter.

Work alongside the business

Stay engaged after closing and help turn the plan into action.

Succession

A transition is about more than price.

For many owners, a business represents decades of work. Employees may have been there for years. Customers know the founder personally. The company's reputation may carry the family name.

Those things matter. We approach succession as both a transaction and an operating transition, with the objective of creating continuity while positioning the business for the future.

Start Early

You do not need to be ready to sell today.

A conversation can begin well before a transaction. If you are thinking about retirement in a few years, wondering whether to sell all or part of the company, or simply want to understand your options, we are happy to start there.

Confidential Conversation

Thinking about what comes next?

Tell us a little about your business and what you are considering. An initial conversation is confidential and does not commit you to a transaction.
