About Newmara

A new age holding company for local operating assets.

Newmara invests in real assets and real businesses. Our focus is simple: own durable companies and properties, improve them with disciplined operations, and hold them with a long term view.

We are not built around short fund cycles or financial engineering. We are built around stewardship, compounding, and hands on ownership.

Our work sits at the intersection of small business, real estate, operations, technology, and patient capital.

Our Approach

01
